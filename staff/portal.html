Token counter

Ask for the token, hand over the dishes that are ready and take the payment. Each dish handed over is one step on the undo history.

    Fresh food

    Each dish has a cooking queue, first in, first out. Mark ready emails people in the order they reserved, skipping cancellations. Leave portions empty to serve everyone waiting.

      Coupons

      Make a code for an occasion: how much it takes off, who may use it and on which days. Each person can use a coupon once a day; it comes off what they pay at the counter. Coupon changes are not on the undo list, so switch a coupon off rather than removing it if you may need it again.
