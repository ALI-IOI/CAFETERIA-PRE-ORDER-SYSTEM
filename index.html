How it works

  1. Reserve before the cutoff

    Pick your dishes on the menu and reserve them before the cutoff. Each person can reserve a few portions of each dish; the menu shows how many.

  2. We cook and email you

    The kitchen cooks what was reserved and emails you when your food is ready, first come, first served.

  3. Collect while it’s fresh

    Pay at the counter when you collect. Food stays fresh for about 7 hours, and we remind you every hour.
