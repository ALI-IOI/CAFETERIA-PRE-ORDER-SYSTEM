How it works

  1. Reserve before the cutoff

    Pick your dishes on the menu and reserve them before the cutoff. Your whole tray gets one token number.

  2. We cook and call your token

    The kitchen cooks what was reserved, first come, first served. When your food is ready, your token moves to Ready on the token board and we email you.

  3. Show your token, collect, pay

    Show the token at the counter and pay as you collect. Food stays fresh for about 7 hours, and we remind you every hour.
